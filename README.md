# Homework Helpers 📚✨
### An Anti-Procrastination Homework Assistant for Struggling Students

**Homework Helpers** is designed specifically from the perspective of a student struggling with procrastination, forgotten assignments, and loomingly close deadlines. Instead of handing over direct answers that cripple exam performance, this app walks students through Socratic step-by-step hint ladders, builds daily accountability with an interactive calendar, and provides empathetic tough-love coaching with bonus points!

---

## 🖥️ Screen Overview & Features

### 🌟 Screen 1: Greetings, Next Due Date & Daily Calendar
* **Empathy-First Greeting**: Acknowledges procrastination and demystifies the anxiety of feeling behind.
* **Urgent Next Submission Callout**: Prominently features the next homework deadline with a live countdown timer (`e.g., Physics due in 18h`).
* **One-Click Direct Action**: Direct button: **"I'm Stuck! Solve My Doubts &rarr;"** that takes the student directly to Screen 2.
* **Interactive Homework Calendar**:
  * Month-by-month grid displaying all homework deadlines with urgency-colored badges (Urgent < 24h, Due this week, Completed).
  * Click on any calendar date to quickly log forgotten or newly assigned homework.
* **Daily Reminders & Check-off System**:
  * Interactive checklist allowing students to check off finished homework.
  * Checking off homework triggers celebratory confetti and awards **+40 points**!
* **Add Homework Quick Form**:
  * Capture Subject, Topic, Due Date, and Effort level (Quick 15 min, Moderate 45 min, Heavy 2+ hours).

---

### 💡 Screen 2: Question & Doubt Solver (Socratic & Non-Spoiler)
* **Post Doubts Easily**:
  * **Text Mode**: Type or paste any math, physics, chemistry, coding, or literature problem.
  * **Scan / Upload Image Mode**: Drag and drop pictures of worksheets, textbook questions, or snap a live photo using the browser camera (`getUserMedia` API).
  * **Sample Presets**: Quick-load Calculus Quotient Rule, Newton's Incline Friction, or Chemistry Stoichiometry.
* **Strict Non-Spoiler Policy**:
  * **Step 1: Formula & Concept Identification**: Breaks down what principles apply (e.g. *Low d-High minus High d-Low*).
  * **Step 2: Component Breakdown**: Differentiates parts or isolates known forces.
  * **Step 3: Setup & Intermediate Guidance**: Walks through algebraic assembly without spoiling.
  * **Step 4: Self-Reflection Challenge**: Prompts the student to evaluate the final step on their own.
* **Interactive Thinking Check**:
  * Test intermediate steps and earn **+50 Bonus Points** for genuine reasoning without cheat codes.
* **Direct Button to Screen 3**:
  * "See Coach's Critique & Claim Bonus Points".

---

### 🏆 Screen 3: Critique, Commentary & Encouragement Vault
* **Coach's Honest Critique (Constructive & Relatable)**:
  * **The Reality Check**: Explains *why* the student procrastinated (dopamine avoidance, task paralysis, fear of starting).
  * **The Encouragement**: Praises what the student got right (not cheating, breaking down the problem, overcoming inertia).
  * **The 30-Minute Action Plan**: Gives clear, immediate micro-tasks.
* **Bonus Points Vault & Levels**:
  * Points earned from logging homework, unlocking steps, checking off tasks, and reasoning through problems.
  * Streak counter (`4 Day Streak`) and Level Progression (`Level 2: Momentum Builder`).
* **5-Minute Procrastination Buster Timer**:
  * Built-in Pomodoro micro-sprint timer with synthesized audio chimes.
* **One-Click Return to Calendar**:
  * Prompt return to Screen 1 to mark homework as complete!

---

## 🚀 How to Run the App

1. Simply double-click or open `index.html` in any modern web browser (Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Firefox, Brave, Safari).
2. No installation, command line, or `npm` build required—everything runs seamlessly client-side!
3. All homework entries, points, and streaks are persisted automatically via browser `localStorage`.
